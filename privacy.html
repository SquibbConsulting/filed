Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

The short version

Filed does not collect, store or share any of your data. Your photos never leave your device.

Your photos

Filed asks for access to your photo library so it can find clutter. All analysis — grouping look-alikes, recognising receipts and documents, reading text in photos, and writing the short summaries — runs on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Filed does not upload photos, thumbnails, text or any results to any server, including ours.

Filed changes your library only when you ask it to: "Set aside" adds photos to a "Filed · Set Aside" album, "Restore" removes them from it, and "Empty Set Aside" moves them to Recently Deleted after Apple's own confirmation.

What Filed keeps on your device

To open quickly, Filed saves its analysis (for example which photos are look-alikes) and your Set Aside list in its own storage on your device. Deleting the app removes it. It is never transmitted.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Filed receives only a confirmation of what you are entitled to; it never sees your payment details or Apple Account.

No tracking, no analytics, no accounts

Filed has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Filed does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
