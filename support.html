Where do set-aside photos go?

Into an album called "Filed · Set Aside" in your Photos library. They stay visible there until you restore them (always free) or empty Set Aside.

How do I get photos back after emptying Set Aside?

Emptying moves photos to Photos → Recently Deleted, where they stay for 30 days. Open Recently Deleted and tap Recover.

Why does Filed never flag some photos?

Photos with people in them and your Favourites are never suggested. Filed looks for screenshots, receipts, documents, signs, photos of screens, bursts, look-alikes and large videos.

Does Filed upload my photos?

No. Everything runs on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Restoring purchases

On the plans screen, tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account. To cancel Keep Clean, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What does the Clean Pass include?

30 days of unlimited setting aside from the day you buy it. It is a single payment and does not renew.
