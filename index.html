Filed

Private. On-device. Undoable.

Filed finds the screenshots, receipts, documents and look-alike shots that pile up in your photo library, files them by year, and lets you clear them calmly. Everything happens on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Nothing is deleted until you say so. Photos you set aside wait in a "Filed · Set Aside" album, and restoring them is always free.

Filed is made by Squibb Consulting LLP, Pune, India.
